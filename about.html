A simpler way for Nigerian sellers to show their products and get orders.

VEACASY is a catalog system for small businesses that sell on WhatsApp and Instagram. You put your products online, share one link, and customers send you their order on WhatsApp.

Why we built it

Many sellers send product photos and prices one customer at a time. It takes hours, and orders get lost in long chats. VEACASY replaces that with one clean link that shows everything you sell and sends you the order in a single message.

It is simple on purpose. No website knowledge is needed, and you can run your store from your phone.

What we do and what we don’t

What VEACASY does

  • Hosts your product catalog
  • Gives you your own store link
  • Sends customer orders to your WhatsApp
  • Shows you visits and product clicks

What VEACASY does not do

  • Take payments from your customers
  • Hold your sales money
  • Process or deliver your orders
  • Ask for your customers’ card details

Your customers pay you directly, the way you already collect payment. VEACASY never stands between you and your money.
