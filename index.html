FAQ

Questions, answered.

The important things before you put your store online.

Do I need technical skills to use VEACASY?+

No. If you can use WhatsApp and Instagram, you already know enough to run your store. The dashboard is designed for sellers, not developers.

How do I receive payments from customers?+

Orders come to you directly on WhatsApp, so you collect payment the way you already do — bank transfer, cash, POS or another method you agree with your customer.

Can I customise how my store looks?+

Yes. You can upload your logo and choose your store color from the dashboard. Your storefront is designed around your business identity.

How does the WhatsApp order button work?+

Each product can open WhatsApp with the product name and price already included in the message, making it easy for the customer to send the order.
