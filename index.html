Questions, answered

The important things to know before you put your store online.

How do I upgrade after the trial?

Choose your plan and message us on WhatsApp. Once your payment is confirmed, we send your upgrade code. Enter it in your dashboard and your plan unlocks right away.

Is the price monthly or yearly?

Yearly. Your first payment covers year one. From year two you renew yearly at a lower price. The Custom plan also has a monthly maintenance fee.

What happens when my trial or plan ends?

Your store shows “temporarily unavailable” to customers. We keep your data for up to 90 days so you can upgrade and come back.

How do customers pay me?

Veacasy does not take orders or payments. Customers send you their order on WhatsApp and you collect payment the way you already do: bank transfer, cash, POS or another method you agree on.

How does the cart work?

Customers add products to a cart and tap Send to WhatsApp. WhatsApp opens with the full list and total ready to send to you.

Do I need technical skills?

No. The dashboard is made for sellers, not developers.

Can I make the store look like my brand?

Yes. Upload your logo, set your store name and get your own store link.
