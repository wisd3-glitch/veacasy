Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This explains what information VEACASY collects, why, and your choices. We aim to follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

What we collect

  • Account details: email, password (stored in scrambled form), business name, WhatsApp number, and optional contact details and address.
  • Store content: product names, prices, descriptions, photos and logo.
  • Basic technical data our hosting records, such as device and IP address, to keep the service secure.

What is public

Your store name, link, logo, products, and contact details you choose to show are visible to anyone with your store link.

How we use it

To run your store, show it to customers, verify support requests, prevent abuse, and contact you about your plan. We do not sell your data or use it for third-party advertising.

Store activity counts

To show sellers how their store is doing, we count visits to a store, taps on products, and taps on “Send to WhatsApp”. These counts are tied to the store and the date, not to the customer. We do not record a customer’s name, phone number or what they type in WhatsApp.

Orders and WhatsApp

When a customer taps "Order on WhatsApp", the conversation happens on WhatsApp under its own privacy rules. We do not read those messages.

Who we share with

Service providers that run VEACASY: Supabase (database, login and file storage) and Vercel (hosting). Their servers may be outside Nigeria. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Password resets

We verify identity with you on WhatsApp before issuing a one-time reset link that expires in 30 minutes.

How long we keep it

While your store is active, and for up to 90 days after your plan ends, then we may delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete your information at any time by contacting us. Deleting your store removes your products and photos.

Children

VEACASY is for people aged 18 and over.

Changes and contact

We will post updates here. Questions: WhatsApp +234 805 620 5515.
